HoPE-CT: how the ₦75,000 household cash transfer works

A plain guide to Nigeria’s Household Prosperity and Empowerment Cash Transfer, and how to collect your payment safely.

How to collectAvoid scams

What is HoPE-CT?

It is a federal government programme that sends cash to poor and vulnerable households. It is run by the Federal Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Reduction through the National Cash Transfer Office (NCTO), under the National Social Safety Nets Project – Scale Up.

The programme is aimed at

  • Low-income and vulnerable households
  • Households on the government’s social protection records

Not sure if your household is listed? Ask your State Operations Coordinating Unit (SOCU) or your local government social welfare office.

How to collect your payment

  1. Look out for your alertSome beneficiaries have reported an SMS about a ₦25,000 payment through a payment provider called TagPay.
  2. Confirm it is realCheck the alert with the NCTO or your bank or payment provider before you do anything.
  3. Follow the official instructionsCollect your money only the way your payment provider or the NCTO tells you. Never let someone else “collect it for you”.
  4. Ask at the right officeFor questions about where or how to collect, visit your State Operations Coordinating Unit (SOCU) or your local government social welfare office.
  5. Wait for the next paymentsThe money comes in three payments. If you are unsure about the next one, ask the NCTO or your SOCU.

No alert yet? Don’t give up. Payments are not sent to everyone at the same time.

Stay safe from scams

  • It is free. Never pay anyone to “register” you or “release” your money.
  • Never share your PIN, OTP or card details with anyone.
  • Don’t click links in messages saying you are “approved” for a grant.
  • Confirm alerts with your bank, payment provider or the NCTO.
  • Report anyone who asks you for money or bribes.

Questions people ask

Is HoPE-CT for every Nigerian?

No. It is for poor and vulnerable households that are already on government lists.

How much will I get?

₦75,000 in total, paid in three payments of about ₦25,000.

Can I apply on this website?

Yes. This website can be used.

Why did someone else get an alert and I didn’t?

Payments are not sent to everyone at once. Ask your SOCU or the NCTO about your status.

Does anyone charge a fee?

No. The programme is free. Report anyone who asks for money.

Do states have their own programmes?

Some do. Cross River and Ogun State have started their own cash support. Ask your state government.
